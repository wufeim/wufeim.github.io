I am a Research Scientist at [Meta Superintelligence Labs](https://ai.meta.com), working on post-training of the Muse models with a focus on long-horizon agentic RL, synthetic data, and RL sandboxes. I have contributed to agentic post-training and evaluation of Muse Glimmer, Muse Spark, and Muse Wonder series models.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Johns Hopkins University](https://www.jhu.edu) in 2026, advised by [Bloomberg Distinguished Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Distinguished_Professorships) [Dr. Alan Yuille](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~ayuille/), where my research focused on multimodal LLMs, 3D spatial reasoning, and video understanding. Before that, I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics, <i>summa cum laude</i>, from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://rpi.edu) in 2020, where I was fortunate to work with [Prof. Bülent Yener](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/~yener/) and [Prof. Lirong Xia](https://www.cs.rpi.edu/~xial/).
